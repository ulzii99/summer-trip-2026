🚗 4 hrs from Bryce (280 mi) 🚶 ~2.5 miles walking

Grand Canyon National Park (Full Day)
Early morning start (beat the heat). Walk Rim Trail sections, venture slightly down Bright Angel Trail (turn around after 1 mile). Afternoon: IMAX theater in Tusayan (air-conditioned break!). Optional: helicopter tour. Complete Junior Ranger badge.

Monument Valley Navajo Tribal Park
Drive through stunning Navajo Nation landscape. Check into The View Hotel (book early!). Take a guided Navajo tour of the valley floor - see iconic mittens, totem poles, and John Ford Point. Watch sunset from your room's balcony.

Morning: Canyonlands Island in the Sky district. Mesa Arch sunrise hike (0.5 mi), Grand View Point. Return to Moab by lunch for pool time and exploring downtown shops. Relaxed afternoon - recharge for Colorado tomorrow.
🚶 ~3 miles walking

Aspen, Colorado
Scenic drive through Colorado mountains via I-70 (beautiful!). Arrive in Aspen mid-afternoon. Evening stroll through charming downtown - window shop, grab dinner. Take it easy - you're at 8,000 ft elevation now!

11

Aspen, Colorado (Full Day)
Take Silver Queen Gondola to top of Aspen Mountain - incredible views! Hike around the summit area. Afternoon: drive to Maroon Bells (shuttle may be required) for easy lakeside walks. Evening: explore more of downtown, ice cream treat.

💡 Pro Tips for June 13-26, 2026

  • Start early: Hit parks by 7-8am to beat crowds and heat (especially Arches, Grand Canyon in mid-June)
  • June weather: Desert parks will be HOT (90-100°F). Colorado will be pleasant (70-80°F days, 40-50°F nights)
  • Hydration: Desert air = dehydration. Drink 1 gallon per person per day when hiking
  • Junior Ranger programs: Free at every national park - your kid can earn 6 badges this trip!
  • Download offline maps: Cell service is spotty - download Google Maps offline for Utah and Arizona
  • Gas up: Fill tank at half - gas stations are sparse between Monument Valley and Moab
  • Altitude: Aspen (8,000 ft) & Rocky Mountain (12,000+ ft) - take it easy, stay hydrated, watch for altitude sickness
  • Monument Valley: No alcohol sold on Navajo land - plan accordingly
  • Book The View Hotel NOW: It sells out 6+ months ahead for summer weekends
  • Delicate Arch: Start the hike by 6pm for sunset - bring headlamps for the walk back in the dark
