📅 Day-by-Day Itinerary

1
Fri, Jun 19
Las Vegas → Grand Canyon
Early morning flight from DCA, arrive Las Vegas ~9:30 AM (local time). Pick up rental car and drive to Grand Canyon South Rim. Check into Tusayan hotel, then head to Mather Point for your first breathtaking canyon views. Watch sunset from Yavapai Point. Pick up Junior Ranger booklet!
✈️ ~5 hr flight 🚗 4.5 hrs to Grand Canyon (280 mi) 🚶 ~2 miles walking
2
Sat, Jun 20
Grand Canyon National Park (Full Day)
Wake up early to beat the heat! Catch sunrise at Mather Point. Walk sections of the Rim Trail (paved, easy). For adventure, hike partway down Bright Angel Trail (turn around after 1-1.5 miles). Afternoon: IMAX theater in Tusayan for an air-conditioned break. Complete Junior Ranger badge!
🚶 ~5 miles walking
3
Sun, Jun 21
Monument Valley Navajo Tribal Park
Drive through stunning Navajo Nation landscape. Check into The View Hotel (book early - this sells out!). Take a guided Navajo tour of the valley floor - see the iconic mittens, totem poles, and John Ford Point where countless westerns were filmed. Watch sunset from your room's private balcony.
🚗 3 hrs from Grand Canyon (180 mi) 🚶 ~2 miles walking
4
Mon, Jun 22
Monument Valley → Bryce Canyon
Optional: wake early for Monument Valley sunrise. Drive through scenic red rock country to Bryce Canyon. Arrive mid-afternoon and explore the otherworldly hoodoos. Walk the Rim Trail between Sunrise and Sunset Points. Stay for the spectacular sunset lighting up the orange spires!
🚗 4.5 hrs from Monument Valley (270 mi) 🚶 ~3 miles walking
5
Tue, Jun 23
Bryce Canyon → Zion National Park
Morning hike: Queen's Garden Trail (1.8 mi) - descend among the fairy-tale hoodoos. Complete Bryce Junior Ranger program. Drive scenic Highway 89 and Highway 9 through red rock tunnels to Zion. Check into Springdale hotel, explore the charming town, grab dinner with canyon views.
🚗 1.5 hrs from Bryce (85 mi) 🚶 ~4 miles walking
6
Wed, Jun 24
Zion National Park (Full Day)
Start early! Take the park shuttle into Zion Canyon. Riverside Walk (2.2 mi round trip, paved, kid-friendly) - wade into the Virgin River at the end for a refreshing splash! Afternoon: Lower Emerald Pools Trail (1.2 mi). Complete Zion Junior Ranger program. Evening pool time at hotel to cool off.
🚶 ~5 miles walking
7
Thu, Jun 25
Zion → Las Vegas
Morning: One more Zion hike - Pa'rus Trail (3.5 mi, flat, paved, stroller-friendly) or revisit a favorite spot. Drive back to Las Vegas through the scenic Virgin River Gorge. Check into Vegas hotel, relax by the pool. Evening: explore Fremont Street Experience with its LED canopy light shows!
🚗 2.5 hrs to Las Vegas (160 mi) 🚶 ~4 miles walking
8
Fri, Jun 26
Las Vegas / Valley of Fire
Option A: Morning trip to Valley of Fire State Park (1 hr away) - stunning red sandstone formations, Fire Wave trail, ancient petroglyphs. Return by early afternoon. Option B: Vegas fun day - hotel pool, Shark Reef Aquarium, or High Roller observation wheel. Pack bags, early bedtime for morning flight.
🚗 Optional: 2 hrs round trip to Valley of Fire 🚶 ~2-3 miles walking
9
Sat, Jun 27
Las Vegas → Home
Morning flight back to DCA. Return rental car at Harry Reid International Airport. Arrive home with incredible memories, 4 Junior Ranger badges, and thousands of photos! Rest up - moving day is Monday! 📦
✈️ ~4.5 hr flight home 🚶 ~1 mile walking

💡 Pro Tips for June 19-27, 2026

  • Start early every day: Parks are HOT in late June (90-105°F). Hit trails by 7-8 AM and be done by noon.
  • Hydration is critical: Desert air = dehydration. Drink 1 gallon per person per day when hiking. Bring electrolytes!
  • Junior Ranger programs: Free at every national park - your kid can earn 4 badges this trip! Great motivation.
  • Download offline maps: Cell service is very spotty. Download Google Maps offline for Arizona, Utah, and Nevada.
  • Gas up early: Fill your tank whenever you're at half. Gas stations are sparse between Monument Valley and Bryce.
  • Monument Valley note: No alcohol sold on Navajo land. Plan accordingly if that matters to you.
  • Book The View Hotel NOW: It sells out 6+ months ahead for summer. This is the #1 priority reservation!
  • Zion shuttle: Private vehicles aren't allowed in Zion Canyon April-October. Use the free park shuttle.
  • Vegas on a budget: Fremont Street is more fun (and cheaper) than the Strip for families!
  • Pack for moving day: You'll be tired when you get home - have easy meals ready and minimize unpacking.
